These are raw counters reported by CPA, not a bill or a complete measure of consumption. Cache and reasoning counters can overlap with input, output, and reported total; they are not additive and are never added here.

CPA delivery has no durable replay guarantee. Split or cumulative streams can omit input/cache or later output; failed or disconnected streams can lose tokens or retain an earlier success. Zero reported usage does not prove zero consumption. Only retained, committed local history is queried.
